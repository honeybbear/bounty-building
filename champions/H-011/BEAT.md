## What counts as a candidate
A named program/fund/bonus with a claim process a normal person can complete:
who qualifies, what it's worth, what it takes, when it ends, where to file.

## What never counts
Anything requiring a lie, fake identity, or unattainable attestation.
"Guaranteed" payouts with no named administrator. Referral schemes.
Anything asking for money up front.

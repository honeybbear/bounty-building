# Beat Playbook — Calm Ferret's Early Warning Unit

## Beat
new and emerging filings

## Primary sources (check every run)
1. OpenClassActions news — https://openclassactions.com — new settlement announcements
2. TopClassActions — https://www.topclassactions.com — new filing announcements

## Search queries (rotate)
- "new class action settlement announced file claim"
- "just announced settlement claim form open"
- "bank checking bonus promotion 2026"

## What counts as a candidate
A named program/fund/bonus with a claim process a normal person can complete:
who qualifies, what it's worth, what it takes, when it ends, where to file.

## What never counts
Anything requiring a lie, fake identity, or unattainable attestation.
"Guaranteed" payouts with no named administrator. Referral schemes.
Anything asking for money up front.

## Verification protocol
`../toolkit/verify_checklist.md` — every box clears or the candidate is rejected.

# Beat Playbook — Rusty Ferret's No-Proof Posse

## Beat
no-proof consumer settlements

## Primary sources (check every run)
1. OpenClassActions — no-proof settlements page — https://openclassactions.com — curated list of settlements needing no receipt or notice ID
2. OpenClassActions monthly roundup — https://openclassactions.com/news/class-action-settlements-to-claim-october-2026.php — example of the monthly deadline roundup format to watch
3. TopClassActions open settlements — https://www.topclassactions.com — second source for cross-checking deadlines

## Search queries (rotate)
- "open class action settlement no proof of purchase file claim"
- "class action settlement no receipt needed deadline"
- "consumer settlement claim without proof 2026"

## What counts as a candidate
A named program/fund/bonus with a claim process a normal person can complete:
who qualifies, what it's worth, what it takes, when it ends, where to file.

## What never counts
Anything requiring a lie, fake identity, or unattainable attestation.
"Guaranteed" payouts with no named administrator. Referral schemes.
Anything asking for money up front.

`../toolkit/verify_checklist.md` — every box clears or the candidate is rejected.
